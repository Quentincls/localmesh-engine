Drop photos anywhere in this window. One goes to Front. Four are matched by view names in the filenames when possible. Otherwise they fill Front, Right, Left and Back in file order.

A clear front photo is enough to start.

Four-view input

More angles give the engine more of the subject’s shape. Use the same subject and similar framing for all four photos.

Standard is a good starting point.

Sets the starting variation. Leave blank for random or reuse a number like 42.

Quality and seed details
Draft
Fast preview, with a budget of about 80,000 faces.
Standard
Recommended for most models, with about 150,000 faces.
High
More sampling and finer detail, with about 250,000 faces.
Max
Highest shape resolution and detail. Targets a GPU with 24 GB of VRAM.
Seed range
Enter 0 to 2,147,483,647 for a fixed seed. Blank or −1 picks a random seed.
